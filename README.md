**GuardSphere**

**Your Guide to a Safer Digital World**

GuardSphere is a responsive cybersecurity awareness website designed to educate users about common cybersecurity threats and provide practical guidance on staying safe online.

The website introduces users to different cyberattacks, explains how they work, and highlights preventive measures that individuals can take to protect their personal information and digital devices.

## 🏆 Achievement

**Merit Award** — Codelympic 2024, Website Design

## 🌐 Features

* **Cybersecurity Homepage** — Introduces common cybersecurity threats and their real-world impact.
* **Evil Twin Attacks** — Explains how malicious Wi-Fi networks impersonate legitimate networks and potentially expose users' data.
* **Zero-Day Exploits** — Introduces vulnerabilities that attackers exploit before a patch becomes available.
* **Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) Attacks** — Explains how attackers can intercept communications between two parties.
* **Practical Security Tips** — Provides recommendations to help users recognise threats and improve their online safety.
* **Responsive Design** — Designed to provide a user-friendly browsing experience across different screen sizes.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Raise awareness of common cybersecurity threats.
* Help users understand how cyberattacks occur and their potential consequences.
* Promote safer online habits through practical prevention strategies.
* Make cybersecurity knowledge more accessible to everyday internet users.

## 👥 Target Audience

GuardSphere is intended for students, everyday internet users, and beginners who want to improve their understanding of cybersecurity without needing an advanced technical background.

## 🔒 Disclaimer

GuardSphere is an educational project intended to promote cybersecurity awareness. The information provided is for learning and preventive purposes and should not be considered a substitute for professional cybersecurity advice.
